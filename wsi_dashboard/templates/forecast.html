How the forecast works
  • Confirmed AR: open invoices whose expected collection date (promise date if set, otherwise due date) is today or in the future.
  • At-Risk AR: open invoices already past their expected collection date but not yet received.
  • AP Outflows: open vendor bills due in the 30-day window plus recurring monthly obligations projected from today.
  • Hard flag (orange) fires when the confirmed-only balance dips below $5,000. Soft flag (yellow) fires when the at-risk scenario causes the dip but confirmed-only stays above buffer.
  • 100% collection is not assumed for at-risk invoices — use the two balance columns to bracket the realistic range.
